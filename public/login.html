Instructions to Candidates
  1. Use the latest version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a laptop or desktop computer. Mobile phones and tablets are not permitted.
  2. A working webcam and microphone are mandatory. Your face must remain clearly visible, and sound around you is monitored, for the entire duration of the test.
  3. You will be asked to share your entire screen. The camera and screen are monitored and recorded throughout the test.
  4. The test opens in full-screen mode. Switching tabs or windows, leaving full-screen, or copying and pasting is recorded as a violation.
  5. The test is submitted automatically when the permitted number of violations is reached or when the time ends.
  6. Do not share your roll number or password. Only one sign-in is allowed at a time.
